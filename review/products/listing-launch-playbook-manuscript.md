# The Listing Launch Playbook

## AI workflow kit for real estate listing marketing

Listinglever helps real estate agents turn high-friction client and marketing
tasks into practical AI-assisted workflows.

This playbook is designed to help you turn raw listing notes into usable
marketing assets: positioning, MLS-style description drafts, social captions,
email copy, neighborhood highlights, buyer-agent talking points, and a final
review checklist.

Important: this product is not legal, tax, accounting, brokerage, MLS, fair
housing, or compliance advice. Review all outputs against your brokerage
policies, MLS rules, state requirements, local regulations, and professional
guidance before publishing.

## Quick start

Use this workflow when you have a new listing and need to create a complete
marketing package without starting from a blank page.

You will need:

- Property facts.
- Seller-approved features and upgrades.
- Professional photos or photo notes.
- Neighborhood and location details.
- Brokerage and MLS requirements.
- Your preferred AI writing tool.

Recommended flow:

1. Gather property inputs.
2. Define the listing angle.
3. Generate MLS-style description drafts.
4. Create social and email copy.
5. Build neighborhood highlights.
6. Prepare buyer-agent talking points.
7. Run the final review checklist.

## Step 2: Define the listing angle

The listing angle is the practical reason a buyer should care. It should be
specific enough to guide the copy but not so narrow that it excludes qualified
buyers.

Common angles:

- Move-in ready convenience.
- Entertaining and indoor-outdoor living.
- Low-maintenance lifestyle.
- Space for working from home.
- Proximity to shops, transit, parks, or local amenities.
- Updated finishes and modern comfort.
- Flexible layout.
- Outdoor space.
- Value in a specific neighborhood or property category.

### AI prompt: generate positioning angles

```text
Act as a real estate listing strategist. Based only on the verified property
brief below, suggest five possible listing angles.

For each angle, include:
1. The buyer situation it may appeal to.
2. The strongest supporting property facts.
3. A one-sentence marketing hook.
4. Any claims that need verification before use.

Avoid:
- Protected-class references.
- Unsupported school, safety, investment, or appreciation claims.
- Overpromising lifestyle outcomes.

Property brief:
[Paste organized property brief.]
```

### Select one primary angle

Choose the angle that is:

- Most supported by facts.
- Most visually obvious in the photos.
- Most likely to matter to buyers in that price range.
- Safe to use under brokerage, MLS, and fair housing guidelines.

## Step 3: Draft MLS-style descriptions

MLS descriptions need to be accurate, concise, and compliant with local rules.
Use AI to draft options, then review carefully.

### AI prompt: MLS description options

```text
Act as a real estate copywriter. Draft three MLS-style property descriptions
using the verified facts below.

Requirements:
- Keep each version under [MLS character limit].
- Use a polished but factual tone.
- Lead with the strongest property angle.
- Mention only verified facts.
- Do not include protected-class references, unsupported neighborhood claims, or
  guarantees.
- End with a simple showing-oriented call to action if allowed.

Primary angle:
[Paste selected angle.]

Verified property facts:
[Paste facts.]
```

### MLS review checklist

Before publishing, check:

- Does every factual claim match the listing data?
- Are measurements, counts, and upgrades accurate?
- Does the copy avoid protected-class references?
- Does the copy avoid unsupported claims about schools, safety, appreciation, or
  investment performance?
- Does it comply with MLS character limits and formatting rules?
- Has the seller or appropriate reviewer approved the key claims?

## Step 4: Create social captions

Use social copy to translate the listing angle into short, skimmable posts.

### AI prompt: social captions

```text
Create social media captions for a new real estate listing.

Inputs:
- Property angle: [Paste angle.]
- Verified property highlights: [Paste highlights.]
- Target platform: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, or short-form video caption.

Create:
1. One short caption under 75 words.
2. One medium caption under 150 words.
3. One caption focused on neighborhood or location context.
4. One caption focused on the standout interior feature.
5. One caption focused on the strongest photo.

Rules:
- Do not invent facts.
- Avoid hype, guarantees, or compliance-risk language.
- Include a simple call to action such as "message me for details" if allowed.
```

### Caption review checklist

- Does the caption match the photo or asset it will accompany?
- Is the call to action allowed by brokerage policy?
- Are hashtags appropriate and not misleading?
- Does the caption avoid exaggerated claims?
- Does the caption still make sense without the full listing context?

## Step 5: Create email announcement copy

Email copy should be useful and direct. Send it to the appropriate audience
based on brokerage policy and your contact list permissions.

### AI prompt: listing announcement email

```text
Draft a listing announcement email for a real estate agent.

Audience:
[Past clients / buyer leads / local contacts / buyer agents / referral partners]

Property:
[Paste verified property facts and selected angle.]

Requirements:
- Subject line options.
- Email under 250 words.
- Helpful, professional tone.
- Three bullet-point highlights.
- Simple call to action.
- No unsupported claims or pressure tactics.
```

### Subject line options

Use or adapt:

- New listing: [property type] in [area]
- Just listed: [short property hook]
- A new [area] listing worth seeing
- New listing with [verified standout feature]
- For buyers watching [area]

## Step 6: Build neighborhood highlights

Neighborhood copy is useful but risky if it drifts into unsupported claims.
Keep it factual and source-aware.

### Safe neighborhood inputs

Use:

- Nearby amenities.
- Distance to major landmarks when verified.
- Local parks, shops, restaurants, or transit access.
- General convenience features.

Avoid:

- School quality claims unless sourced and allowed.
- Safety claims.
- Demographic references.
- Claims about future appreciation.
- Statements that imply who should live there.

### AI prompt: neighborhood highlights

```text
Create neutral neighborhood highlight copy for a real estate listing.

Use only these verified details:
[Paste verified nearby amenities, distance notes, transit, parks, shops, or
other factual details.]

Create:
1. Three short listing-friendly bullets.
2. One paragraph under 75 words.
3. One social caption.

Avoid:
- Protected-class references.
- Safety, school-quality, appreciation, or demographic claims.
- Claims that are not supported by the provided details.
```

## Step 7: Prepare buyer-agent talking points

Buyer-agent talking points help communicate what is notable about the listing
without forcing every detail into the public description.

### AI prompt: buyer-agent sheet

```text
Create a concise buyer-agent talking point sheet for this listing.

Include:
- Top three reasons buyers may want to see it.
- Key property facts.
- Standout features to notice during a showing.
- Questions buyer agents may ask.
- Items that should be verified through official documents or disclosures.

Rules:
- Use only verified facts.
- Do not make legal, inspection, valuation, or financing claims.
- Keep it skimmable.

Property brief:
[Paste property brief.]
```

## Final review questions

Ask before publishing:

1. What claim is most likely to be challenged?
2. What fact needs one more verification?
3. Does the copy reflect the actual photos and condition?
4. Is the strongest buyer benefit visible in the first image or first sentence?
5. What should be removed because it is nice but not necessary?

## Reuse workflow

For the next listing:

1. Save the final listing brief.
2. Note which prompts worked best.
3. Save the approved description and captions.
4. Record any compliance feedback.
5. Use that feedback to improve the next listing workflow.

The goal is not just faster copy. The goal is a repeatable listing launch
system that gets easier every time.
