# Free Listinglever Buyer Consultation Prep Checklist

Use this checklist before a buyer consultation to prepare the conversation,
organize key questions, and avoid starting from a blank page.

Important: this checklist is not legal, tax, accounting, brokerage, or
compliance advice. Review buyer agreement language, compensation discussions,
and client-facing materials with your broker, attorney, MLS, and state-specific
resources.

## 1. Before the meeting

Gather:

- Buyer name and contact details.
- Desired location or neighborhood.
- Timeline.
- Budget range, if known.
- Financing or pre-approval status.
- Must-haves and deal breakers.
- Prior agent relationship, if any.
- Questions the buyer already asked.

## 2. Questions to clarify

Ask:

1. What is prompting the move?
2. What timeline are you hoping for?
3. Have you spoken with a lender?
4. What areas are you considering?
5. What would make a home a clear yes?
6. What would make a home a clear no?
7. Have you bought a home before?
8. What concerns do you have about the process?
9. How do you prefer to communicate?
10. What would make this process feel successful to you?

## 3. Topics to prepare

Be ready to explain:

- How the buying process typically works.
- What you do before, during, and after showings.
- How you help evaluate homes.
- How offers, negotiations, inspections, and deadlines work.
- What buyer agreements are and why they matter.
- How compensation is discussed and documented.
- What the buyer should review before signing anything.

## 4. Value explanation prompt

Use this prompt with your AI assistant:

```text
Help me draft a clear, client-friendly explanation of the value I provide as a
buyer's agent.

Context:
- Market: [market]
- Buyer type: [first-time buyer, move-up buyer, relocation buyer, investor]
- Tone: helpful, direct, and not pushy

Include:
- Search strategy
- Showing preparation
- Offer guidance
- Negotiation support
- Timeline management
- Communication
- Risk and deadline awareness

Avoid:
- Legal advice
- Guaranteed outcomes
- Claims that compensation is fixed or non-negotiable
```

## 5. Meeting agenda

Suggested agenda:

1. Confirm goals and timeline.
2. Explain the buying process.
3. Discuss search criteria.
4. Explain your role and services.
5. Discuss buyer agreement requirements and next steps.
6. Discuss compensation at a high level, using approved brokerage guidance.
7. Confirm immediate next actions.

## 6. Follow-up checklist

After the meeting:

- Send recap email.
- Send any required brokerage documents.
- Confirm next showing or search step.
- Record buyer criteria in CRM.
- Note unanswered questions.
- Schedule follow-up if the buyer is not ready.
