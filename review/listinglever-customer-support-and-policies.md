# Listinglever Customer Support and Policy Drafts

Use these drafts as starting points for Etsy, Gumroad, and customer replies.
Review them before publishing.

## Store policy principles

- Be clear that products are digital downloads.
- Do not promise business results, leads, clients, or legal compliance.
- Make refund terms fair while accounting for instant digital delivery.
- Direct buyers to review all real estate language with their broker, MLS,
  attorney, or compliance contact.

## Etsy FAQ drafts

### Is this a physical product?

No. This is a digital download. No physical item will be shipped.

### Do I need ChatGPT or another AI tool?

Yes. These kits are designed to be used with an AI writing tool such as ChatGPT,
Claude, Gemini, or another comparable assistant. The product provides the
workflow, prompts, structure, and review steps.

### Is this legal advice or NAR guidance?

No. These products are not legal, tax, accounting, brokerage, compliance, or NAR
guidance. They are practical workflow templates. Review all language with your
brokerage, MLS, attorney, or state-specific compliance resources before using it
with clients.

### Can I edit the templates?

Yes. The workflows are designed to be adapted to your market, brokerage style,
property details, and client process.

### Can I share this with my team?

One purchase is intended for one agent or internal team. Do not resell,
redistribute, or repackage the files as your own product.

## Refund policy draft

Because these are instant-access digital downloads, all sales are generally
final once files are delivered.

If you have trouble accessing the files or believe you purchased the wrong item,
contact us and we will help resolve the issue.
