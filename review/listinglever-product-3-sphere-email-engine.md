## Workflow

### Step 1: Segment the sphere

Group contacts into:

- Past clients.
- Warm buyer leads.
- Warm seller leads.
- Referral partners.
- Local community contacts.
- Cold or stale contacts.

For each group, note:

- Relationship strength.
- Last meaningful contact.
- Likely next useful topic.
- Whether the contact should receive personal outreach or a general newsletter.

### Step 2: Choose the monthly theme

Pick one primary theme per month:

- Local market update.
- Home maintenance reminder.
- Seller preparation tip.
- Buyer planning tip.
- Neighborhood spotlight.
- Rate or affordability explainer.
- Referral appreciation.
- Client success story.
- Seasonal move planning.
- Tax or insurance reminder with appropriate disclaimers.

### Step 3: Generate the first draft

Prompt:

```text
Act as a real estate marketing assistant. Draft a warm, useful email for
[audience segment] about [monthly theme].

Requirements:
- Keep it under 250 words.
- Use a helpful, non-pushy tone.
- Include one practical tip.
- Avoid guarantees, fear-based claims, or legal/financial advice.
- End with a soft invitation to reply.

Local context:
[Add city, market condition, season, or neighborhood detail.]
```

### Step 4: Personalize by segment

Prompt:

```text
Create three versions of this email:
1. Past clients.
2. Potential sellers.
3. Referral partners.

Keep the core topic the same, but adjust the opening, example, and call to
action for each audience.
```

### Step 5: Review before sending

Check:

- Is every claim accurate?
- Does it avoid steering, protected-class references, and unsupported market
  claims?
- Does it comply with brokerage, MLS, state, and email-marketing requirements?
- Does it sound like a helpful agent, not a mass-marketing blast?
- Is the reply ask simple?

### Step 6: Follow up

After replies, sort contacts into:

- Needs personal response.
- Wants valuation or buyer consult.
- Possible referral source.
- No action.

## Ten email templates

### 1. Past client check-in

Subject: Quick home check-in

Hi [First Name],

I was thinking about your home and wanted to send a quick check-in. If you have
made any updates, repairs, or plans for the next year, it may be a good time to
refresh your home file so you have a clearer picture of where things stand.

No pressure at all, but if you want, reply with any updates you have made and I
can help you think through what they may mean for future resale positioning.

Hope you are doing well,

[Agent Name]

### 2. Home maintenance reminder

Subject: A useful home reminder for this month

Hi [First Name],

This month is a good time to check [seasonal maintenance item]. It is a small
task, but it can help prevent bigger issues later and keep your home in better
shape over time.

If you want, I can also send a short seasonal home-maintenance checklist you can
keep on hand.

Best,

[Agent Name]

### 3. Seller planning email

Subject: Thinking about selling later this year?

Hi [First Name],

If selling is even a possibility later this year, the best time to prepare is
before you need to move quickly.

The highest-leverage early steps are usually:

1. Reviewing likely buyer expectations in your area.
2. Identifying small improvements that may affect presentation.
3. Understanding your likely pricing range before making plans.

If you want a simple prep checklist, reply "seller checklist" and I will send
one over.

[Agent Name]

### 4. Buyer planning email

Subject: A smoother way to start a home search

Hi [First Name],

If buying a home is on your radar, the process is easier when you clarify your
budget, timeline, must-haves, and financing early.

Even if you are not ready to tour homes yet, a short planning conversation can
help you understand what to prepare and what questions to ask next.

If helpful, I can send a buyer planning checklist.

[Agent Name]

### 5. Referral appreciation email

Subject: Thank you for keeping me in mind

Hi [First Name],

I just wanted to say thank you for keeping me in mind when real estate comes up
with friends, family, or coworkers.

Referrals are never expected, but they are always appreciated. If someone you
know needs a helpful starting point, I am happy to answer questions or point
them in the right direction.

Thanks again,

[Agent Name]

### 6. Local market explainer

Subject: A quick note on the local market

Hi [First Name],

I wanted to share a quick local market note. In [area], the biggest thing I am
watching right now is [market factor].

For sellers, that may affect [seller implication].

For buyers, that may affect [buyer implication].

Every situation is different, so treat this as a general update rather than
personal advice. If you want to talk through what it means for your plans, just
reply here.

[Agent Name]

### 7. Stale lead reactivation

Subject: Still thinking about a move?

Hi [First Name],

We connected a while back about real estate, and I wanted to check in. Are you
still thinking about buying, selling, or just keeping an eye on the market?

If plans changed, no problem. If it is still on your radar, I can help you get a
clearer next step without pressure.

Best,

[Agent Name]

### 8. Listing follow-up to sphere

Subject: New listing you may want to see

Hi [First Name],

I wanted to share a new listing at [property/location] because it may be useful
to you or someone you know.

Quick highlights:

- [Highlight 1]
- [Highlight 2]
- [Highlight 3]

If you know someone looking in this area, feel free to forward this along or
reply with questions.

[Agent Name]

### 9. Neighborhood spotlight

Subject: Neighborhood spotlight: [Neighborhood]

Hi [First Name],

This month I am highlighting [Neighborhood]. People are often drawn to it
because of [feature], [feature], and [feature].

If you are curious about homes in that area or want a quick comparison with
another neighborhood, I am happy to help.

[Agent Name]

### 10. Soft annual review

Subject: Want a simple home value check?

Hi [First Name],

Many homeowners only check their home value when they are ready to sell, but it
can be useful to understand the range even if you are staying put.

If you want, I can prepare a simple home value check using recent local activity
and current market context.

No pressure either way.

[Agent Name]
