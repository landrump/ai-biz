# Founder-Edge Alternative Backlog

Listinglever is the first experiment, but the broader business model should
also test offers that use the founder's stronger edge in BizOps, strategy,
financial modeling, and process design.

## Alternative thesis

Sell productized AI workflow kits for consultants, agencies, fractional
operators, and small professional-services firms.

These buyers may pay more than individual real estate agents because the
workflow kits can connect directly to client delivery, operations, reporting,
and revenue retention.

## Selection criteria

Prioritize ideas that:

- Solve a recurring business operation problem.
- Can be delivered as templates, SOPs, prompts, trackers, and examples.
- Do not require custom implementation to be useful.
- Can be sold through Gumroad, a simple landing page, or low-touch outbound.
- Use the founder's credibility in operations, strategy, financial modeling, or
  process design.

## Candidate offers

### 1. Client Reporting Workflow Kit

Buyer:

- Consultants.
- Freelancers.
- Small agencies.
- Fractional operators.

Problem:

Client updates are inconsistent, overly manual, or too vague to build trust.

Product promise:

Turn scattered project notes into a clear weekly client update with executive
summary, progress, risks, decisions needed, and next steps.

Included assets:

- Weekly client update template.
- AI prompt sequence for turning notes into updates.
- Risk and blocker language bank.
- Decision-needed escalation template.
- Client-facing email copy.
- Internal review checklist.

Launch price:

$49-$99.

Why it fits:

This uses BizOps and executive communication skills and targets buyers with a
clear business reason to pay.

### 2. SOP Builder Workflow Kit

Buyer:

- Agency owners.
- Operations managers.
- Small-business owners.
- Virtual assistant teams.

Problem:

Processes live in people's heads and are hard to delegate.

Product promise:

Turn messy process notes into clear SOPs, checklists, role responsibilities, and
training handoffs.

Included assets:

- SOP structure.
- Process interview script.
- AI prompt sequence.
- Quality-control checklist.
- Delegation handoff template.
- Notion or Google Docs organization map.

Launch price:

$49-$149.

Why it fits:

SOPs are a direct match for process design and can be sold as a higher-value
workflow product than many marketplace PDFs.

### 3. Financial Model Review Kit

Buyer:

- Founders.
- Solo consultants.
- Small-business operators.

Problem:

Financial models are hard to review for logic, assumptions, scenarios, and
decision usefulness.

Product promise:

Use an AI-assisted review workflow to pressure-test a simple business forecast
before making pricing, hiring, or investment decisions.

Included assets:

- Assumption audit checklist.
- Scenario planning prompts.
- Revenue-driver map.
- Cost-driver map.
- Model review memo template.
- Decision summary template.

Launch price:

$79-$199.

Why it fits:

This is closer to the founder's financial modeling edge and may support a
higher perceived value.

### 4. Agency Onboarding Workflow Kit

Buyer:

- Small agencies.
- Freelance service providers.
- Fractional service businesses.

Problem:

Client onboarding is inconsistent, creating delays, unclear expectations, and
extra support work.

Product promise:

Create a repeatable client onboarding system with intake, kickoff, access
collection, success criteria, and first-week communication.

Included assets:

- Intake form template.
- Kickoff agenda.
- Access checklist.
- Client expectations email.
- Internal onboarding checklist.
- First-week update template.

Launch price:

$49-$149.

Why it fits:

It is concrete, recurring, and close to revenue operations.

## Recommended parallel test

Prepare the **Client Reporting Workflow Kit** as the first founder-edge
alternative while Listinglever launches.

Reasons:

- It is easy to explain.
- It is useful across multiple professional-services niches.
- It can be built quickly from the same workflow-kit format.
- It has a clear buyer pain: keeping clients informed and reducing churn risk.

## Evidence to gather next

- Gumroad and Etsy examples of client reporting templates.
- LinkedIn or forum language around client-update pain.
- Pricing for consulting templates, SOP kits, and agency operating systems.
- Whether buyers prefer Google Docs, Notion, Excel, or PDF delivery.
