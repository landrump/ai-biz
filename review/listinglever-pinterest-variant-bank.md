# Listinglever Pinterest Variant Bank

Use these pin concepts for organic testing. Each pin should link to the most
relevant Etsy listing, Gumroad page, or landing page.

## Board structure

Create boards around buyer intent rather than only the brand:

- Real Estate Agent Marketing Ideas
- Realtor Buyer Consultation Tools
- AI Tools for Real Estate Agents
- Real Estate Listing Marketing
- Realtor Email Templates
- Real Estate Business Systems

## Testing notes

- Start with five pins: two listing launch, two buyer consultation, one free
  checklist.
- After seven days, create variants based on the best saves and outbound clicks.
- Track pin title, overlay text, destination, impressions, saves, outbound
  clicks, and sales or email signups.
