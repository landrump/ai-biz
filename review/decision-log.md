# Decision Log

This log records major business decisions, the evidence behind them, and what
would change the decision.

## 2026-04-26: Select productized B2B AI workflow kits as core model

Decision:

- Build a productized B2B AI workflow-kit business rather than a generic prompt
  pack shop, custom agency, or fully automated content business.

Reasoning:

- Digital products fit the starting budget and low-touch fulfillment goal.
- Etsy and Gumroad support fast launch without custom software.
- Workflow kits are more differentiated than generic prompts because they
  package the full job-to-be-done.
- The founder's BizOps, strategy, financial modeling, and process-design
  background is a meaningful advantage for workflow-product creation.

Evidence:

- Etsy supports low-cost digital product listings with a $0.20 listing fee and
  transaction-based fees.
- Gumroad supports no-monthly-fee digital product sales.
- Marketplace evidence shows real estate agents and small businesses already buy
  templates, marketing kits, SOPs, and workflow assets.
- NAR settlement practice changes create timely conversation and workflow needs
  around buyer agreements and compensation communication.

What would change this:

- No traffic after adequate listing and channel testing.
- Traffic but no evidence of interest after title, thumbnail, preview, and price
  tests.
- Stronger demand signal for a founder-edge operations workflow product.

## 2026-04-26: Keep Listinglever as Experiment 1

Decision:

- Use Listinglever as the first market experiment for real estate agent AI
  workflow kits.

Reasoning:

- Assets already exist or are partially specified.
- The niche has visible paid template demand.
- Buyer consultation and listing launch are specific, high-friction workflows.
- The brand can stay faceless and marketplace-led.

What would change this:

- Account setup is delayed long enough that a parallel founder-edge test can
  launch faster.
- Early listing metrics show no traffic despite keyword and pin iteration.
- A founder-edge operations product gets stronger pre-launch signal.

## 2026-04-26: Prioritize launch readiness over more product creation

Decision:

- Prepare the first two products and bundle for publication before building a
  larger catalog.

Reasoning:

- More internal assets do not prove demand.
- The nearest revenue milestone is live purchase pages, not a larger product
  library.
- Product 3 should be briefed, but not allowed to delay launch of products 1 and
  2.

What would change this:

- Account setup remains blocked and there is spare agent capacity.
- A small Product 3 asset can be created without slowing launch.
- Marketplace results show a gap that Product 3 directly fills.
