# Listinglever Launch Command Center

This is the control document for moving Listinglever from prepared assets to a
live, measurable business.

## Current launch objective

Launch Listinglever as a faceless digital product brand selling AI workflow kits
for real estate agents.

Primary promise:

> AI workflow kits that help real estate agents turn time-consuming business
> tasks into clear, copy-ready execution systems.

Initial products:

1. **The Listing Launch Playbook**
2. **The Buyer Consultation Kit**
3. **The AI Real Estate Workflow Bundle**

Primary channels:

1. Etsy for search-driven marketplace discovery.
2. Gumroad for direct checkout and future email capture.
3. Pinterest for visual discovery and long-tail traffic.

## Owner-only tasks

These require identity, payment, business, or account access and cannot be
completed by the agent in this environment.

### Required before sales

1. Buy or confirm the domain:
   - Active: `listinglever.net`
2. Confirm production inbox preference.
   - Current mailbox: `info@listinglever.net`
   - Optional alias later: `hello@listinglever.net`
3. Change any temporary mailbox password created during setup and store it only
   in a password manager or other secure location.
4. Finish Etsy seller setup if any payment, tax, shop policy, or listing fields
   remain incomplete.
5. Finish Gumroad setup:
   - Confirm profile details.
   - Confirm payout/payment setup.
   - Create product shells or provide setup screenshots.
6. Create Pinterest business account.
7. Set up business payment account or payout method where still required.
8. Share or upload the customer-facing launch files into the Google Drive folder:
   - https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1WnlKTfb-9uQHYt09OvYJ8EhW5ioC8KDH
9. Confirm the legal seller name and tax/payment details.

### Required before publishing

1. Review public claims for accuracy.
2. Confirm product files are final.
3. Confirm refund policy language.
4. Confirm whether Etsy Offsite Ads should be disabled while under the threshold
   where opt-out is available.
5. Upload product files and images.
6. Publish listings.

## Minimum viable launch

Do not wait for a full catalog. The minimum viable launch is:

1. One individual product live on Etsy.
2. One bundle or second product live on Etsy.
3. Matching Gumroad page for the strongest offer.
4. Five Pinterest pins pointing to the marketplace or Gumroad product.
5. A simple tracker for impressions, clicks, favorites, carts, and sales.

## Launch acceptance criteria

Listinglever is considered launched when:

- At least two paid products are live.
- A buyer can purchase and automatically receive the files.
- Product pages include clear outcome, contents, usage instructions, and
  disclaimers.
- Traffic and sales metrics can be reviewed after launch.
- A decision log records what was launched and why.
