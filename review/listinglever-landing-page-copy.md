No. Listinglever products are digital downloads.

Yes. The kits are designed to be used with an AI writing assistant such as

### Are these legal or brokerage forms?

No. These are workflow and drafting templates. They do not replace your
brokerage forms, MLS rules, state requirements, legal guidance, or compliance
review.

### Can I customize the outputs?

Yes. The workflows are intended to be adapted to your market, property, client,
voice, and brokerage requirements.
