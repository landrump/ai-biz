# Listinglever Marketplace Assets

These assets are drafted for the first launch of Listinglever as a productized
B2B AI workflow-kit experiment for real estate agents.

## Brand positioning

Listinglever helps real estate agents turn high-friction client and marketing
tasks into practical AI-assisted workflows.

Core promise:

> AI workflow kits for real estate agents who want copy-ready client,
> marketing, and follow-up systems without starting from a blank page.

Avoid claiming:

- Guaranteed leads.
- Guaranteed sales.
- Legal compliance.
- NAR approval or endorsement.
- Replacement for broker, attorney, MLS, or state-specific guidance.

## Launch notes

- Publish the two individual products first.
- Publish the bundle immediately after the individual products.
- Link each individual product to the bundle in the description.
- Use the same visual style across all listings so the shop feels coherent.
- Do not activate paid ads until the listings have enough organic impression and
  click data to establish a baseline.
